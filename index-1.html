Find your Team ID one-time

Open the official FPL app or site while logged into your account, go to Points or Pick Team, and look at the page address bar: fantasy.premierleague.com/entry/1234567/event/5 — the number right after /entry/ is your Team ID.

That's all this needs. It's public information (the same way your overall rank is public to anyone), so there's no password to type and nothing to log into — just that one number, once.
